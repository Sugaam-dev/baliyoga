# Bali Yoga Kendra

> Yoga Alliance Certified Yoga Teacher Training Courses (YTTC) & Transformative Wellness Retreats in Bali, Indonesia, and global spiritual centers.

Bali Yoga Kendra is an internationally recognized Yoga Alliance certified yoga school offering authentic 50 Hour, 100 Hour, 200 Hour, 300 Hour, and 500 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Courses (Multi-Style, Hatha, Ashtanga, Kundalini, Yin, Aerial, Prenatal, and Sound Healing), alongside holistic wellness retreats, sound bath healing, meditation, and cultural immersion programs in Ubud, Bali, with partner retreat hubs in Mysuru, Rishikesh, Chiang Mai, and Dharamshala.
